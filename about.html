Hi, I’m Sheena!

I’m a fourth-year product design student at UT Austin who loves simplifying complex ideas. I'm especially interested in how people think, make decisions, and interact with systems, and I enjoy turning those insights into experiences that feel natural, clear, and impactful.

What I enjoy most about the design process is finding the pain points and figuring out how to fix them. I like understanding the why behind things, like why people get stuck, what they actually need, and why a process works the way it does, so the solution solves the real problem.

Outside of work, you’ll find me playing tennis, running, hiking, traveling, drawing, or trying a new cafe.
